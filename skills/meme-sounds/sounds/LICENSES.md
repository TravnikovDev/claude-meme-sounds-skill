# The sounds in this folder

All four are original: synthesised from rules (Faust models played through DawDreamer), with no
recording in them. They are released under CC0 1.0: use them anywhere, for anything, with no credit.

| file | length | what it is |
|---|---|---|
| `train.mp3` | 24.0 s | a subway interior: rumble, the wheels' double clack every 1.2 s, a pole rattle |
| `dogs.mp3` | 13.0 s | dogs barking far off down a street, as a doorbell microphone hears them |
| `chime.mp3` | 1.5 s | a two-note ding-dong, E5 then C5, on two bells; no brand's chime |
| `notice-tone.mp3` | 3.4 s | three bursts of two bare sines over one held 580 Hz tone, a notice from nowhere |

Nothing in `catalog.yaml` is in this folder. Those recordings belong to their owners and are fetched
from their source pages by `scripts/fetch.py`.
